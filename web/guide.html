Computer / laptop

  • Left side poora menu dikhta hai.
  • Same kaam — sirf buttons zyada dikhte hain.
App Chrome / Safari mein kholo. Phone pe “Add to Home Screen” se icon bana sakte ho.

0 Pehli dafa setup

  1. Link kholo: https://buyappsc.web.app
  2. Apna email / password se login karo.
  3. Agar naya business banana ho: type Transport choose karo.
  4. Currency PKR rakho.

Login ke baghair data save nahi hota. Har staff ko apna account / access chahiye (office se).

1 Menu — har button ka matlab

Gari Khata Har gaari ka alag sheet (jaise Excel tabs: C-2319, TTA-598). Kharcha, Jama, Total Baqaya.
Company Bills Rozana log: Date · Gaari No · Weight (kg) · Company. Yahan paisa nahi lagta.
Month Hisaab Mahine ke end: total kg × rate/ton = company bill + har gaari pe Jama.
Company Rates Company ka default rate per ton save — Month Hisaab mein auto aata hai.
Customers Companies add (A & S Oil Mills wagaira) + company khata / payment.
Drivers Driver names (optional) — payroll ke liye.

2 Gaari add karo

  1. Gari Khata kholo.
  2. Neeche + dabao.
  3. Name aur Registration number likho — jaise C-2319 ya TTA-598.
  4. Save.
Tip: Registration number wohi likho jo Excel / number plate pe hai — list mein wahi dikhega.

Gaariyan ke beech swipe

List se ek gaari kholo. Upar “Gaari 1/3 — swipe left/right” dikhega. Left / right swipe se agli / pichhli gaari khul jati hai — Excel tabs jaisa.

Khata kholna

  1. Gari Khata list se gaari pe tap.
  2. Upar bada number = Total Raqam / Baqaya (running balance).

Opening (pichla baqaya)

  1. Pehli dafa: AppBar pe Opening dabao.
  2. Pichla baqaya likho. Agar udhaar / minus ho to - use karo.
  3. Opening ek dafa set hota hai.

Kharcha likhna

  1. Neeche Kharcha dabao.
  2. Date, Tafseel, Raqam likho.
  3. Jaldi ke liye neeche chips dabao — Local kharcha, Diesel, Peshgi, Driver salary, Commission, Mistri…
  4. Save.

Jama likhna

  1. Neeche Jama dabao.
  2. Jab paisa aaye / bill receive ho (manual entry).
  3. Note: Month Hisaab ke baad company bill ka Jama khud gaari pe lag jata hai — woh entry yahan se change nahi hoti.
Total ka formula: pehle wala total − Kharcha + Jama.
Balance minus (negative) bhi ho sakta hai — Excel jaisa.

Date filter + company payment

  • Date filter se sirf ek range dikhao.
  • AppBar pe payment icon → company choose → company khata (payment receive).

6 Month Hisaab (mahine ka settle)

Excel jaisa: saare kg jodna × rate per ton = bill.

  1. Month Hisaab kholo.
  2. Mahina + Saal choose karo.
  3. Company choose karo.
  4. Rate per ton check karo (Company Rates se aata hai; change kar sakte ho).
  5. App dikhati hai: total kg + bill preview.
  6. Settle Month dabao.
  7. Confirm dialog aayega — kg × rate = bill padh kar Haan, Settle.
Settle ke baad do cheezein hoti hain:
• Company ke khate mein bill (udhaar / receivable)
• Har related gaari ke Gari Khata mein Jama (us gaari ke kg ke hisaab se)
Ek company + ek mahina = ek hi dafa settle. Dubara settle nahi hota.
Us mahine ki weight entries Settled ho jati hain — edit/delete band.

Settle ke baad

  • Print / PDF bill — company ko dena / record.
  • Company payment likho — jab paisa aa jaye.

10 Offline + sync

  • Internet na ho to upar peela banner: “Internet nahi — data phone pe save… online hone pe sync.”
  • Offline bhi bills / khata likh sakte ho — phone pe save rehta hai.
  • Online hone pe cloud pe backup / sync ho jata hai.
  • Sync Status (More / sidebar) se pending sync dekh sakte ho.
App “kharab” nahi — aksar sirf offline hoti hai. Banner dekho, kaam jari rakho.

Roz ka short rule

  1. Trip / load hua → Company Bills mein weight (kg) likho.
  2. Kharcha hua (diesel, local, peshgi…) → us gaari ke Gari Khata mein Kharcha.
  3. Mahina khatam → Month Hisaab → confirm → Settle.
  4. Company ne paisa diya → Payment likho.
  5. Zarurat ho to Print / PDF nikaal lo.

Masle / sawalat

Weight galat likh diya?

Agar Pending hai → bill pe tap karke edit. Agar Settled hai → change nahi; office se check.

“Pehle gaari / company add karein”?

Pehle Gari Khata mein gaari, Customers mein company, phir bill.

Month Settle reject / error?

  • Us mahine us company ke pending weight hone chahiye.
  • Rate per ton zero nahi hona chahiye.
  • Pehle se settle ho chuka ho to dubara nahi.

Gari pe Jama khud aa gaya?

Haan — Month Settle ke baad. Woh entry lock hai (Excel ke month bill jaisa).

Internet nahi / data gayab lag raha?

Peela offline banner check karo. Login same account se karo. Sync Status dekho. Phir bhi masla ho to WhatsApp support.
